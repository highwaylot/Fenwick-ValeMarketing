HIGHWAYLOT redesign · concept 04

The Window Sticker

Every screen is a white card with an ink header strip, ruled spec blocks and a total line at the bottom, the way a new-car window sticker is laid out, without ever drawing one.

Palette weight in this concept. White and paper carry the pages, ink carries the header strips, yellow is spent once per screen.

  • Card#FFFFFF leads, every sticker
  • Paper#FAFAF6 page behind the stickers
  • Ink#1B2431 nav, header strips, totals
  • Line#DEDBD1 the rules
  • Steel#5B6472 labels, fine print
  • Yellow#F5B700 one action or one number per screen; the landing field

Green #2F6B4F appears once, on the "No, it's in good shape" answer. Yellow dark #8A6600 is not needed: yellow never sits under small text here.

Landing

The yellow field and the Anton wordmark stay. The two ink cards become one sticker with three ruled rows; the third is greyed and honest.

Why it's different

  • The numbers are laid out like a bill: label on the left, figure on the right, total at the bottom. A buyer already knows how to read it.
  • Yellow is spent once per screen. The button on the form, the total on the result, the one price in the wikiLOT headline.
  • Every page is white paper with an ink strip across the top, so the landing, the form and the guide look like one printed set.
  • The form becomes four blocks instead of eleven loose fields, and the estimate sits where a total sits, so you can see it filling in.
  • No shapes, no icons beyond the shield. The rules do the work.

What was kept from the handoff: the all-yellow landing field with Terms and Privacy inside it, the Anton wordmark, the ink nav with the shield mark, the hero headline with the real number inline, yellow-fill primary button with ink text, 8px radius and soft shadow on cards, hover lift, the Inter form fields and every figure and field label from the live pages.

What I changed: cards go from ink to white with an ink header strip; the two landing cards become one card with ruled rows plus a greyed "Buy and sell · Next" row; the Value My Car hero folds into the sticker header; the form is grouped into four blocks with the estimate as a total line; the wikiLOT diagonal hero and stat widget become a flat ink band with the two figures in the headline; make cards become ruled price lists and the coloured make avatars are gone; the yellow rule under the nav is dropped so yellow stays reserved.
