Why it's different

  • Numbers are the only thing in color. One yellow stroke per screen marks the figure to read first; everything else is ink on paper.
  • It looks like a page from a ledger, not a brochure. Hairline rules and right-aligned tabular figures do the organizing, not cards or bands.
  • wikiLOT is a real table. Twenty-five makes scan top to bottom in one column of prices, with models tucked under each make.
  • Value My Car is a worksheet with its running figure pinned at the top, so the dollar line is never off screen while you fill it in.
  • Calm by default. No diagonal band, no colored avatars, no shadows. The shield mark and the Anton wordmark carry the brand on their own.
Kept from the handoffPalette and the three fonts as assigned; the shield mark; the Anton wordmark as one word; the thin ink nav bar with a yellow underline on the active link; yellow-fill primary button; the hero headline with its real number inline; every figure, field and line of copy from the live pages; Terms and Privacy on the landing; the footer line.
What I changedLanding moves from an all-yellow field to paper with a highlight band through the wordmark, and the two cards become two ledger lines plus one "next" line for buying and selling. Ink hero bands become ruled paper blocks. Form boxes become underlined fields in a single column with the live figure pinned on top. wikiLOT's body-style cards become a slim strip and its make cards become a sortable-looking table with model sub-rows. Green is not used because no positive state exists on these screens.
