Why it's different

  • The lane line from the shield is the page structure, not a decoration: one dashed rule, content on either side of it.
  • On an ink page the only yellow things are numbers, the lane line and the one button, so the price is what you read first.
  • Panels are 1px lines on slightly lighter ink. No shadows, gradients, glow or motion beyond a short fade on load.
  • The form's live figure sits in a rail that stays on screen while you fill the fields, instead of a line that scrolls away.
  • The landing keeps today's yellow field; every page after it is ink, so the two tools feel like one instrument.

Handoff

What was kept: the yellow landing field with the Anton wordmark, the two ink cards and Terms/Privacy inside the field; the ink nav with the shield; the hero headline with $38,800 avg. inline; the "At 5 years old" widget with the Kia Soul and Escalade figures; the same body-style and make figures; every form field, its order and the "Get my estimate" button; the tagline's "buying and selling are next" as the one marketplace mention.

What I changed: tool pages go ink end to end; the diagonal hero and the yellow nav stripe are replaced by a straight band with one vertical dashed lane line; cards lose their shadows for 1px borders on lighter ink; the live preview moves into a sticky rail; the result page is a single figure on the line; notes on ink use paper at 72% instead of steel, since steel on ink is about 2.6:1.
