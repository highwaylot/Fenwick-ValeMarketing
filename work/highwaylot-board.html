Why it's different

  • Yellow leads on every page, not just the landing. The tool pages open on a yellow band, so all three screens read as one site.
  • The price is the biggest thing in every cell. Make name second, model list third. A buyer reads numbers before words.
  • One wordmark moment: Anton, once, on the landing. Everywhere else the shield and Oswald keep the nav small.
  • No icons, no colored initials, no illustrations. The make board is type, rules and dotted leaders.
  • The lane line shows up once, faint, behind the wikiLOT hero, and nowhere else.

What was kept from the handoffInk nav with the yellow rule, the "$38,800 avg." hero line, 8px ink cards with the soft shadow and 2px hover lift, yellow-fill/ink-text primary buttons, Terms and Privacy inside the yellow field with no dark footer bar, every form field and every figure exactly as live.

What I changedThe landing's two cards merged into one ink board with a real figure on each row; the Value My Car hero flipped from ink to yellow with the number set large; wikiLOT's diagonal ink/yellow split and stat widget replaced by a yellow band whose headline carries the Kia Soul and Escalade figures; the make cards replaced by a five-across price board with dotted leaders and no initials circles; a single result board for the estimate.
