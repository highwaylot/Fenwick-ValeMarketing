Terms

Last updated October 23, 2026.

Fenwick and Vale is a one-person business in Cocoa Beach, Florida. These are the terms for any work it does. They're short on purpose, and they match what the rest of this site says.

Prices

The prices on this site are the prices. Same price for everyone. Jobs over $7,500 get a written quote by email before any work starts.

Paying

Website builds: 50% to start, the rest at launch. Monthly plans (the care plan, profile and reviews, ads management) are billed monthly. Ads management has a $500 setup fee and a 90-day first term, then runs month to month. Ad spend is paid by you, directly to Google, on your own account.

What you own

The domain, the website and every Google account are in your name from day one. If you leave, every login is handed over the same day. There is no hostage hosting and no fee to take your site elsewhere.

Month to month

Care plans and Google work run month to month. Either side can end them with 30 days' notice, by email. No twelve-month contracts.

No guarantees

No ranking, traffic, lead or sales numbers are promised. Nobody can promise a Google ranking. What's promised is the work described on the page, done on time, with a one-page report each month for the monthly plans.

Reviews

Review requests follow Google's rules and the FTC's: Google's own link, every customer asked the same way, no payment or gifts, no asking only the happy ones, no reviews from you, your staff or your family. Fenwick and Vale won't do any of that, for any client.

Your content

You supply the words, photos and logos you want used, or approve the ones written for you, and you confirm you have the right to use them. Timelines start the day your content is in hand.

Showing the work

Fenwick and Vale may link to a finished site from this one, unless you ask it not to.

Limits

Fenwick and Vale is responsible for the work it agrees to in writing, and will fix work that's wrong. It isn't responsible for outages or changes at other companies (hosts, domain registrars, Google), for content you supply, or for how you run your business. The most Fenwick and Vale owes for any job is what you paid for that job.

Law

Florida law applies. Any dispute goes to the courts in Brevard County, Florida.

Changes

These terms can change. The date at the top says when they last did. The terms that apply to a job are the ones in effect when it was agreed.
