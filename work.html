Designs

Four directions for HighwayLot, three for FleetCleared. Each opens as a full page: the screens, the palette and the reasoning. Click the picture.

  • HighwayLot The Board →

    Every page is set like the price board at the front of a used-car lot: yellow field, ink type, big numerals, and the price is always the loudest thing in view.

  • HighwayLot The Ledger →

    Paper leads and ink writes; every screen is a ruled page, and the one number that matters gets a yellow marker stroke.

  • HighwayLot The Highway →

    Ink leads, and the dashed lane line from the shield becomes one vertical rule down every page, with content either side of it like mile markers and the numbers in yellow.

  • HighwayLot The Window Sticker →

    Every screen is a white card with an ink header strip, ruled spec blocks and a total line at the bottom, laid out the way a new-car window sticker is.

  • FleetCleared The Dispatcher →

    A status board for small carriers: five palettes with a live switcher, one English/Spanish and day/night control on every screen, nine website screens and nine social pieces.

  • FleetCleared The Sign Painter →

    A painted road sign carried across the whole site: a green plate with white slab letters, six social posts, and the deep pages in English and Spanish with the switch between them.

  • FleetCleared The Swiss →

    Eight colors, one typeface and a ledger grid, tested on the crowded screens: a directory you can compare three consultants in, a sixteen-rule guide and a ten-square result.

The designs are demos, not live sites. Each keeps its own typefaces, so those pages load fonts from Google; the rest of this site does not.
