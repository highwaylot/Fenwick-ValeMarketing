Privacy

Last updated October 23, 2026.

This site collects nothing about you. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no forms and no accounts. Fenwick and Vale doesn't know who visits.

The only data: email you choose to send

Every contact point on this site is an email link to hello@fenwickvale.com. If you write, Fenwick and Vale gets your email address and what you wrote, and uses them to reply and to do the work you ask for. Nothing is sold, shared or added to a mailing list. If you ask for your emails to be deleted, they are.

One setting, kept on your device

If you pick a trade in the showroom on the Websites page, your browser remembers the choice so the colors stay the same from page to page. That is stored by your browser on your own device, not sent anywhere, and it isn't a cookie. Picking "Back to the Fenwick and Vale look" clears it.

What loads from other companies

One thing loads from another company when you open a page:

  • Hosting. The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host, its servers see the normal connection details (your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for) and may keep them in server logs for a short time. Fenwick and Vale doesn't use or look at them.

The typefaces are served from this site, not from Google. The one exception is the archived first version of the site, under /v1/, which still loads its two typefaces from Google Fonts; that request is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Links

Links to other sites go where they say. Those sites have their own rules.

Changes

If any of this changes, this page changes, and the date at the top moves.
