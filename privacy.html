Privacy

Last updated October 24, 2026.

This site collects nothing about you while you read it. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no accounts. Fenwick and Vale doesn't know who visits.

The only data: what you choose to send

Every contact point on this site is either an email link to hello@fenwickvale.com or the short request form. If you write, Fenwick and Vale gets your email address and what you wrote, and uses them to reply and to do the work you ask for. The request form stores the details you type (name, phone or email, business, note, and which page you were on) in this site's own server storage, used only to reply, and deleted on request. Nothing is sold, shared or added to a mailing list. If you ask for your emails or your request to be deleted, they are.

What loads from other companies

One thing loads from another company when you open a page:

  • Hosting. The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host, its servers see the normal connection details (your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for) and may keep them in server logs for a short time. Fenwick and Vale doesn't use or look at them.

The typefaces are served from this site, not from Google. The exception is the seven design demos under /work/, which load their own typefaces from Google Fonts; those requests are covered by Google's privacy policy.

Links

Links to other sites go where they say. Those sites have their own rules.

Changes

If any of this changes, this page changes, and the date at the top moves.
