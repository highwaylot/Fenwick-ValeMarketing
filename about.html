Fenwick and Vale is a one-person shop. I build websites and set up Google for small businesses in Brevard County, and I build and run three sites of my own: HighwayLot, MOSTMATH and FleetCleared. I started this because the owners I meet keep getting sold things they don't need by people they can't reach afterward. I'd rather do fewer jobs at a fair price and still have you as a client in five years.

What you pay for is judgment, the copy, and a person in Brevard who answers.

The shop is veteran-owned. That's a fact about who you're dealing with, not a reason to hire me.

The deal

Eight promises, kept in writing.

  • Prices are on the page. Same price for everyone.
  • You own the domain, the site and every Google account from day one.
  • Care plans and Google work are month to month, 30 days' notice.
  • No ranking guarantees. Nobody can promise a Google ranking, and anyone who does is lying.
  • A one-page report every month, in plain English.
  • I answer within one business day.
  • If a $29-a-month site is enough for you, I'll say so.
  • I don't sell marketing to businesses listed on FleetCleared. That directory has to stay neutral.
